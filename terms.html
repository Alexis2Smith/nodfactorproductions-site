Nod Factor Radio Text Messaging Program

Nod Factor Radio, operated by Nod Factor Productions and Media, offers two distinct, optional text-message programs: (1) marketing and promotional messages such as station promotions, show and event news, merchandise offers, and giveaways; and (2) informational messages about song submissions, submission status, and related non-promotional service updates.

Opt-In and Separate Consent

Consent for marketing texts and consent for informational/song-submission texts are collected with two independent checkboxes. Both start unchecked. Checking one does not authorize messages from the other program. You can join either program, both, or neither. Text-message consent is not a condition of purchasing products, submitting songs, receiving a giveaway, or using our services. Acceptance of these Terms or our Privacy Policy does not itself enroll you in either text program.

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.
